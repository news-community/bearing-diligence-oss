Open a folder of documents

Choose a folder of meeting records from a body you follow: a council, a school board, a utility. Agendas, packets and minutes are usually on its website; save the PDFs into one folder. Every PDF and text file in it, and in its subfolders, is read on this computer, one at a time, and you can ask about each one as soon as it is added. Anything in a subfolder named private is kept as your own notes. To add a document later, put it in the folder.
