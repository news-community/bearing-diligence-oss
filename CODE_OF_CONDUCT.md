# Code of conduct

Be kind, be specific, and assume good faith. Criticise work, not people. Harassment, personal attacks
and publishing anyone's private information are not tolerated.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this, and may block people who
do so repeatedly. Report a problem privately to the maintainers through GitHub, or by the security
reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if it is sensitive.
